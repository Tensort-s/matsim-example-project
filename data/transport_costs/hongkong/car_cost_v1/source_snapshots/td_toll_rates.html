Time-varying Tolls

The three Road Harbour Crossings (namely the Cross Harbour Tunnel, the Eastern Harbour Crossing and the Western Harbour Crossing) implemented Time-varying Tolls on 17 December 2023. For details, please refer to the "Time-varying Toll Plans Summary Table". In parallel, the "Time-varying Toll Online Enquiry webpage" is also launched to facilitate the public to access.

Tai Lam Tunnel also implemented Time-varying Tolls on 31 May 2025. For more details, please refer to thematic page of Toll Plans for Tai Lam Tunnel to learn more.

& HKeToll has been implemented. Vehicle owners who have used the tunnels must pay the toll within 14 business days after the date of using these tunnels. A $175 initial surcharge for each trip of late payment will be imposed to vehicle owners who have not paid the outstanding tolls. If the vehicle owner does not pay the unpaid toll and the initial surcharge in full by the expiry of 21 calendar days after the date on which a surcharge notice is served, a further surcharge of $350 will be imposed. The penalty for evading tolls is a fine of $5,000 (per trip).

# Please note that the motorist only needs to cover the toll tag with a piece of metallic material such as aluminum foil (also known as tin foil) on the outside of the vehicle being towed.
